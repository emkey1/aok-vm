# AOK VM privacy policy

AOK VM collects no personal data, and has no accounts, analytics, advertising
or tracking of any kind.

- **Your machines stay on your device.** Their disks, settings and logs are
  files in AOK VM's own storage (and, if you turn on a machine's "Share
  /AOK/persist", iSH-AOK's shared folder on the same device). AOK VM sends
  none of them anywhere.
- **What AOK VM fetches from the network, and only when you ask for it:** its
  list of ready-made machines and the machines you choose to download, from
  GitHub (github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com); a machine you add from a
  link you open. GitHub sees the request, as any web server does.
- **Your machines' own network traffic** is theirs: a machine reaches the
  network through your device's connection, as any app's traffic does, and
  AOK VM neither records nor inspects it.
- **Crash reports** reach the developers only if you share them yourself, or
  through Apple if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers
  (Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements).

AOK VM is a fork of UTM, made by the iSH-AOK project. Questions:
https://github.com/emkey1/aok-vm/issues
